POSITION / 01

What happens after a place has stopped behaving as intended?

IRIBIOPOLIS investigates the afterlife of places: material failure, alternative use, ecological occupation, memory and changing ground. Design begins by finding what is already happening before deciding whether to intervene, intensify, redirect or leave a process alone.
