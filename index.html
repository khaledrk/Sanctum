01 / POSITION

Nothing ends where it breaks.

EREÍPIAPÓLIS investigates ruinous landscapes: places altered by decay, abandonment, destruction, flood, occupation and ecological succession. The practice asks what remains, what changes, and what kinds of life continue after the intended life of a place.
