03

For the political, cultural and historical work performed by ruins.

04

Tanya Whitehouse — How Ruins Acquire Aesthetic Value

For the changing perception and representation of modern ruins.

05

Hanna Katharina Göbel — The Re-Use of Urban Ruins

06

Victor Turner — The Ritual Process

For liminality as a condition between established structures.
