PROJECT / 01

RUINS
REGROUNDED

SHORNEMEAD FORT / GRAVESEND / THAMES ESTUARY

Ruins Regrounded began with a walk from Gravesend through its industrial edge and marshes to Shornemead Fort, where decay coexists with graffiti, informal occupation and ecological life. Repeated visits and a field-recording device were used to map ruination as Decay, Subversion and Assimilation. These conditions generated three design responses: Continuation, Acceleration and Cohabitation. Projected toward 2100 and rising tides, selected structures are removed, opened or retained while new circulation and habitat are introduced. The fort becomes a changing social and ecological gathering ground for people, water, vegetation and other forms of life.
