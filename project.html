02 / PROJECT

RUINS
REGROUNDED

Shornemead Fort and the Thames marshes become a changing civic and ecological ground. Twelve openings connect enclosure to tide, sediment, vegetation, informal occupation and public movement. The proposal works through continuation, acceleration and cohabitation: retaining active traces, directing selected forms of decay and building conditions for more-than-human use. The fort remains unfinished and available to change.

DESIGN LOGIC

CONTINUATION / ACCELERATION / COHABITATION

Continuation keeps active traces and public routes. Acceleration directs decay, flooding and succession where they can produce new conditions. Cohabitation gives people, plants, animals, water and weather space to alter the project.
