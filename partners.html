THE WORK NEEDS OTHER KNOWLEDGE.

EREÍPIAPÓLIS is built with people who live, maintain, study, remember, regulate, fabricate and occupy changing spaces.

01 / COMMUNITIES + SPACE USERS

Memory, daily routes, informal repair, conflict, care and knowledge carried through use.

02 / ECOLOGISTS + RESEARCHERS

Hydrology, succession, contamination, species, archives, oral histories and long-term observation.

03 / MAKERS + TECHNICAL COLLABORATORS

Fabrication, material testing, structural judgement, surveying, sound, film and environmental modelling.

04 / PUBLIC + HERITAGE BODIES

Access, stewardship, policy, funding, public programmes and the capacity to hold work over time.
