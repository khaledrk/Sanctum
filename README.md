## Conceptual calibration
V6 was rebuilt after reviewing:
- Georg Simmel, "The Ruin": the ruin retains human form while natural forces act through it; the tension remains legible.
- Anna Lowenhaupt Tsing, The Mushroom at the End of the World: patchy assemblages, disturbance, open-ended temporal rhythms and collaborative survival.
- The Geologic Imagination (Sonic Acts): deep time, Earth-scale forces and the Anthropocene.
- Caitlin DeSilvey / curated decay: care, loss and material transformation.
- Curious Archive, "The Most Powerful Type of Worldbuilding": layered ruins as questions and environments that digest previous worlds.
- Sarah Davis Baker, "The Persistence of Decay" / early "This Too Shall Rot": decay across physical, ecological and digital time.

Visual atmosphere was studied from Scavengers Reign, Annihilation, GRIS, Disco Elysium and BLAME!.
No copyrighted film/game/video imagery is included in this website. Their visual principles were used only as critical references.

## Practice-writing calibration
Writing was studied from Formafantasma, Material Cultures, Public Works Studio, Rotor, Plan Común, TEPETL and J&L Gibbons.
The resulting copy uses direct identity statements, concrete verbs, clear fields of work and explicit collaboration roles.
